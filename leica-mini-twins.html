The Leica Mini and its cheaper twins: same camera, almost 4× the price

When we asked Reddit for cheap alternatives to the Olympus mju II, several people pointed to the same little camera sold under different names: the Minolta Riva Mini (also called the Freedom Escort or Pico), the Olympus Trip AF Mini and the Panasonic C-625AF Super Mini. The Leica Mini is widely reported to be based on it. We checked about 250 eBay listings to see what each version really costs.

Are they really the same camera?

Mostly, yes. Reviewers and owners describe the Minolta, Olympus and Panasonic versions as the same body with small differences, such as the front grip and the position of the LCD. All three use a 34mm f/3.5 lens. The Leica Mini is said to be based on a Minolta Riva model, but it has its own 35mm f/3.5 Elmar lens with a different coating. We couldn't find an official manufacturing record, so treat this as well-documented collector knowledge, not a confirmed fact.

Listings to skip (or read very carefully)

One Japanese seller, about 40 identical listings. "Minolta Pico Freedom Escort Riva LEICA MINI II copy" appears again and again at €198–€263 with shipping. That's 1.5–2× the price of a Riva Mini in Germany, plus possible import fees.

Trip AF Mini at €234–€275. One seller lists five Trip AF Minis at around five times the UK price. Same camera as the €48 one.

"Leica Mini" in a Panasonic title. Two UK listings for the Panasonic C-625AF add "Leica Mini" to the title and ask €299–€353. You can find the same camera for €104.

The Leica Mini 3 is a different camera. It has a 32mm Summar lens and sells for €280 and up. Searching "Leica Mini" also brings up tripod heads, Sofort instant cameras and tiny Sharan collector models that only look like a Leica.

Cheap with a catch. The cheapest Leica Mini in Germany (€111) says "BITTE LESEN!" ("please read"), and a €26 Trip AF Mini in the UK says "No Flash". Read the description before you buy.

Not every "Trip" is a Trip AF Mini. The Trip AF, Trip AF Super, Trip S and Trip Junior are different cameras that show up in the same search.

Which one should you buy?

  • Cheapest: Olympus Trip AF Mini. Same body, same 34mm lens as the Minolta, often under €60.
  • EU buyer, no import fees: Minolta Riva Mini or Panasonic C-625AF from a German or French seller, €104–€130.
  • You want the red dot: Leica Mini from €178. You pay for the name and the Elmar lens, not a different camera body.
