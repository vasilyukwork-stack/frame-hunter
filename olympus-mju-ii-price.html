Listings to skip (or read very carefully)

"mju II 80 / 115 / 170 VF" is a zoom camera. These are a different, cheaper model with a slower zoom lens, mostly sold from Japan for €185–€300. Many titles don't say "zoom", so look for those numbers.

"mju II 35mm f/3.5" doesn't exist. The mju II has an f/2.8 lens; f/3.5 is the older mju I. We found several Japanese listings with this mix-up in the title, so ask which camera it really is.

"Olympus MJU II Champagne 35mm Film Camera with 35mm f2.8 Lens". The same title appears again and again from sellers in China, at €282–€328 with shipping. They may be fine, but make sure the photos are of the camera you'll get.

Keyword-stuffed titles. Listings like "Mju 1 35mm 3.5 … Mju 1 2 I II" are mju I cameras at mju II prices (€179–€189). A mju I usually costs about half that.

Cheap with a warning. The cheapest German listing (€176) says "Bitte lesen!!!" ("please read"), and a UK one at €270 has a broken battery lid. Read the description before you bid.

How to tell it's a real mju II

  • The lens says 35mm 1:2.8 and doesn't zoom.
  • It has a sliding clamshell cover that switches the camera on.
  • In the US it's sold as the Stylus Epic. Same camera, different name.
  • The LCD on top should show the frame counter. A faded LCD is common and can be replaced.
