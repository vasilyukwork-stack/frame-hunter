Nikon L35AF vs Pentax PC35AF vs Ricoh FF-70: cheap f/2.8 point-and-shoots, real prices in 2026

When we asked Reddit what to buy instead of an Olympus mju II, these three kept coming up: simple 1980s autofocus compacts with a sharp 35mm f/2.8 lens. We pulled about 350 eBay listings from six European countries, added shipping, converted to euros and read every title to remove manuals, winders, adapters and other models.

Listings to skip (or read very carefully)

Nikon L35 TW and L35 AW are different cameras. The TW has a two-step 38/65mm lens and the AW is the waterproof version. Both show up when you search "L35AF". The L35AD is the date-imprint version of the L35AF.

"Pikaichi" from Japan at €180–€400+. 36 of the Nikon listings we found were from Japan, typically around €270 with shipping. Many are in great condition, but the same camera costs about €84–€125 in Europe.

Keyword-stuffed titles. One Czech seller lists a Pentax PC35AF-M at €234–€289 with "Nikon L35AF Canon" in the title. A tested Pentax costs €81 in the UK.

Accessories that look like cameras. The Pentax PC35 Winder (€37–€63), Ricoh TC-9 tele adapters, Nikon mini tripods and manuals all come up in these searches. Check the photos.

Other Ricoh FF models. The FF-7, FF-9, FF-10, FF-700 and FZ-70 are different cameras. The FF-9 has an f/3.5 lens, and the FF-700 sells for €80–€156, more than an FF-70.

Untested and "with issues". Several German listings say "ungetestet" or "ungeprüft" (untested), and the cheapest FF-70D from Japan says "Tested with Issues". That's a gamble, not a deal.

Which one should you buy?

  • Best value: Pentax PC35AF. Same 35mm f/2.8 idea as the Nikon, usually around €100, and plenty of tested ones in the UK.
  • The cult classic: Nikon L35AF. Look for the L35AF2 or the ISO 1000 version if you shoot faster film. Under €130 in Europe is a fair price.
  • Cheapest, if you find one: Ricoh FF-70, around €60–€75. Rare in Europe, so grab a tested one when it appears.
