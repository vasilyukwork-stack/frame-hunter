Listings to skip (or read very carefully)

"Olympus XA 2", "XA 1", "XA 3" are different cameras. We found 25 listings where a space hides the model number, from €66 to €294. The XA2 and XA3 have a simpler f/3.5 lens with zone focus, and the XA1 has a fixed-focus f/4 lens. All are cheaper than the XA, so don't pay XA prices for them.

"Olympus XA … mju ii lens". Some UK titles add "mju ii" for search traffic. The XA has its own F.Zuiko 35mm f/2.8 lens; it isn't the mju II lens.

Flash only. The A11 and A16 flashes are often listed as "for Olympus XA" (about €43 for an A11). Check the photos: some listings are only the flash or only the case.

Japanese listings at €200–€385. Most are in good condition and often come with the A11 flash, but the typical one costs about €60 more than in Europe, plus possible import fees.

How to tell it's the real XA

  • The lens says F.Zuiko 35mm 1:2.8. The XA2 and XA3 say D.Zuiko 1:3.5.
  • It has a small rangefinder patch in the viewfinder and a focus lever under the lens.
  • There's an aperture slider on the front, from f/2.8 to f/22. The other XA models don't have one.
  • The shutter button is a flat, very light red touch button. A light seal replacement is a common and cheap fix.
