Is it free?

Yes. The weekly email is free.

How do you make money?

Some links in the email are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the marketplace may pay us a small commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it never changes which deals we pick.

Do prices include import taxes?

Not yet. Buying from outside your country (for example from the UK into the EU) can add VAT and customs fees. We mark cross-border deals so you can check.
