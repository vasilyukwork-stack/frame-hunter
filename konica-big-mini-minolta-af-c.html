Konica Big Mini and Minolta AF-C prices in 2026: which "Big Mini" to buy, and what to pay

Two more pocket cameras our Reddit readers recommended as mju II alternatives. We pulled about 300 eBay listings from six European countries, added shipping, converted everything to euros and read every title. The big finding: "Big Mini" is a name on about ten different Konica cameras, and most of the cheap ones aren't the one people mean.

If you live in the EU, a UK purchase may add about 20–23% import VAT, and Japan can add VAT and customs fees too.

Which Big Mini is "the" Big Mini?

The one people recommend is the small black Big Mini with a fixed 35mm f/3.5 lens: the BM-201, the BM-300/BM-301 "HG" and the BM-302. They're close relatives with small differences, and all are sought after for their sharp lens and close focusing. The zoom models (BM-311Z, BM-510Z, BM-610Z), the APS-film BM-S 100, the Big Mini Jr. (BM-20) and the Big Mini VX (BM-701) are different, cheaper cameras that share the name.

Listings to skip (or read very carefully)

Zoom "Big Minis" from €22. We found 45 listings for the BM-311Z, BM-510Z, BM-610Z, BM-S 100 (APS film), Jr. and VX. They're fine cameras, but not the prime-lens Big Mini, so don't pay Big Mini prices for them.

"AS-IS" Big Minis from Japan at €86. One seller lists the BM-301 again and again in six languages ("AS-IS", "WIE BESEHEN", "TEL QUEL"). It means the camera only powers on and nothing else was checked. Others say "Flash NG", which means the flash doesn't work.

One Minolta AF-C, many listings. A German seller lists the AF-C at least seven times in different languages at €181–€205. Some are "selection" listings where you pick one of several cameras. A tested AF-C costs about €112 in the UK.

Not every Minolta "AF" is an AF-C. The AF-E, AF-EII and AF-DL (€11–€27) are simpler cameras. "Minolta 7000 AF + C" is an SLR, and "Soligor AF C/D" listings are lenses.

Wild prices. Some German AF-C listings ask €250–€350, and one UK seller wants €896 because of a "special" serial number.

Which one should you buy?

  • Faster lens for less: Minolta AF-C. A 35mm f/2.8 in a tiny body, around €100–€112 tested in the UK.
  • The cult classic: Konica Big Mini BM-201, BM-300/301 or BM-302. A tested one from Germany for about €100 is a good price; €180 is typical.
  • On a tight budget: a zoom Big Mini for €30–€60 is a fine snapshot camera, just know it's a different model.
